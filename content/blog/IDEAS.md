# Blog ideas

A backlog for the monthly article. Pick one, or let the monthly task pick the most
relevant one. Cross an idea off (or move it to "Written") once it's published.

Focus areas: **AI · Next.js · React · Storyblok** — written from the perspective of a
frontend developer who cares about CSS, accessibility and calm, maintainable websites.

## What's worth writing about (and why)

- **Your own experience beats news.** A post about something you *did* — a bug you
  fixed, a migration, a component you're proud of — is more useful (and more yours)
  than a summary of a release note. Use news as the hook, your experience as the body.
- **Show code.** Short, real snippets from your projects make posts shareable.
- **Bridge worlds.** You work with ExpressionEngine *and* modern headless stacks. Posts
  that compare the two, or translate ideas between them, are rare and valuable.
- **CSS is your superpower.** Deep dives into layout, typography or animation details
  stand out in a sea of framework posts.
